A ride-hailing app calculates trip fare based on distance and whether the trip happened during peak hours. Peak hours increase demand, so surcharge applies.

Rules:

Base fare ₹50
First 10 km → ₹12 per km
Beyond 10 km → ₹15 per km
Peak hours → 20% extra on total fare

Input:

Distance in km (integer)
Peak hour (Yes/No)

Validations:

Distance cannot be negative

Expected Output:

Distance: <km> km
Peak Hour: <Yes/No>
Total Fare: ₹<amount>
