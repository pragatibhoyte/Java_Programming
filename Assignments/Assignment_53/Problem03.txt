3. Warehouse Stock Management

A warehouse has a product in stock. Every time a customer places an order, the system must check if enough stock exists.

If possible, fulfill the order and update the remaining stock. If the stock becomes very low, show an alert.

Rules:
If requested quantity > available stock → Order fails.
Otherwise, deduct the requested quantity from the available stock.
If remaining stock < 5 → Print a low stock alert.
Input:
Current stock (integer)
Requested quantity (integer)
Validations:
Stock cannot be negative.
Requested quantity must be > 0.
Expected Output:

If successful:

Order Processed Successfully.
Remaining Stock: <value>

If remaining stock is less than 5, also print:

Low Stock Alert!

If failed:

Order Failed: Insufficient Stock.
